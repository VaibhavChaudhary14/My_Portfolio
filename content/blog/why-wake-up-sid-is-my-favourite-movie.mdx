# Why Wake Up Sid Is My Favourite Movie

<Callout type="tip" title="THE CORE THESIS">
Being confused isn't the same as being finished. Growing up isn't about suddenly having a five-year plan or instant certainty — it's about direction, awareness, and having the courage to simply start trying things.
</Callout>

<div className="p-6 my-8 border-l-4 border-amber-500 bg-amber-500/10 rounded-r-lg font-serif text-lg sm:text-xl font-medium leading-relaxed dark:text-amber-200">
  There are movies you watch once.<br/>
  There are movies you watch when you have nothing better to do.<br/>
  And then there are movies you come back to when life starts feeling a little confusing.<br/><br/>
  <strong>For me, <em>Wake Up Sid</em> is the third kind.</strong>
</div>

I don’t think *Wake Up Sid* is my favourite movie because it has the best plot ever written. 

It doesn’t.

It’s a pretty simple story.

A confused 21-year-old guy doesn’t know what he wants to do with his life. He spends his days playing video games, partying, spending his father’s money and generally avoiding the question that every Indian parent eventually asks:

<div className="my-6 p-4 border-2 border-black dark:border-venom-slime bg-yellow-100 dark:bg-yellow-950/50 rounded-lg text-center font-bold text-lg sm:text-xl shadow-neobrutalism-sm text-black dark:text-yellow-300">
  💬 “Beta, life mein karna kya hai?”
</div>

And honestly?

**That’s exactly why I love it.**

Because Sid doesn’t suddenly wake up one morning with a five-year plan.

He just starts trying things.

And I think that’s something we don’t talk about enough.

<div className="p-4 my-6 border-l-4 border-black dark:border-venom-slime bg-black/5 dark:bg-white/5 font-serif text-xl sm:text-2xl font-bold text-center">

Sid doesn’t have an answer.

**And neither did I.**

Maybe that’s why the movie feels so personal.

<SectionBanner 
  act="ACT II" 
  number="02" 
  title="Sid Isn’t Lazy. He’s Lost." 
  subtitle="The crucial difference between being directionless and being lazy" 
  color="blue" 
/>

This is probably the biggest thing I took away from the movie.

At the beginning, Sid looks like the stereotypical irresponsible rich kid.

- He doesn’t study.
- He doesn’t care about his exams.
- He doesn’t take his internship seriously.
- He fights with his father.
- He spends most of his time doing absolutely nothing productive.

But underneath all of that, I don’t think Sid is simply lazy.

<div className="my-6 p-6 border-4 border-black dark:border-venom-slime bg-blue-50 dark:bg-blue-950/40 rounded-xl shadow-neobrutalism text-center">
  <span className="text-2xl sm:text-3xl font-black uppercase text-blue-800 dark:text-blue-300 block mb-2">He’s directionless.</span>
  <span className="font-semibold text-zinc-700 dark:text-zinc-300">There is a fundamental difference.</span>
</div>

When you don’t know what you want, even simple things become difficult.

You procrastinate. You scroll. You play games. You sleep.  
You tell yourself you’ll figure it out tomorrow.

And then tomorrow becomes next week.  
And next week becomes another year.

<Callout type="warning" title="The Real Fear">
<strong>The scary part isn’t failing.</strong> It’s spending years doing things without ever asking yourself whether you actually want them.
</Callout>

Aisha is one of the reasons the movie works so well.

She moves to Mumbai because she wants to become a writer. She doesn’t have everything figured out either.

But she has something Sid doesn’t:

<div className="p-4 my-6 border-3 border-black dark:border-venom-slime bg-pink-100 dark:bg-purple-950/50 rounded-lg text-center shadow-neobrutalism-sm">
  <span className="text-xl sm:text-2xl font-black text-pink-700 dark:text-pink-300">Not Certainty. Direction.</span>
</div>

And I love that distinction.

She isn’t some perfectly successful person who arrives to teach Sid how to live. She’s figuring out her own life too.

The difference is that **she’s willing to move.**

<div className="grid grid-cols-2 sm:grid-cols-3 gap-3 my-6 text-center font-bold text-xs sm:text-sm">

  <div className="p-3 border-2 border-black dark:border-venom-slime bg-white dark:bg-zinc-900 rounded shadow-neobrutalism-sm">⚡ She makes mistakes</div>
  <div className="p-3 border-2 border-black dark:border-venom-slime bg-white dark:bg-zinc-900 rounded shadow-neobrutalism-sm col-span-2 sm:col-span-2">🚀 She keeps going</div>

And slowly, Sid starts doing the same.

He picks up a camera. He starts taking pictures. He notices things differently.

And eventually, he realises:

<div className="my-6 p-4 border-2 border-black dark:border-venom-slime bg-emerald-100 dark:bg-emerald-950/60 rounded-lg text-center font-mono font-bold text-lg text-emerald-900 dark:text-emerald-200 shadow-neobrutalism-sm">

It’s about becoming **aware**.

And maybe that’s what adulthood actually is. **Not having all the answers. Just becoming more conscious about the questions you’re asking.**

Every time I watch *Wake Up Sid*, I notice something different.

- **At 18:** I saw a fun coming-of-age movie.
- **Later:** I saw a story about finding a career.
- **Now:** I see something slightly different.

<div className="p-6 my-8 border-4 border-black dark:border-venom-slime bg-yellow-100 dark:bg-yellow-950/50 rounded-xl shadow-neobrutalism text-center">

  </span>
  <span className="text-sm font-medium text-zinc-700 dark:text-zinc-300">
    You can be 21, 24, 27 or 35 and still not know exactly what you’re doing. That’s okay. The important part is not staying there forever.
  </span>

<div className="my-8 p-6 border-2 border-black dark:border-venom-slime bg-white dark:bg-zinc-900 rounded-xl shadow-neobrutalism space-y-3 font-semibold text-sm sm:text-base">
  <div className="flex items-center gap-3"><span className="text-amber-500">👉</span> <span>Try something. Fail at it. Change your mind. Start again.</span></div>
  <div className="flex items-center gap-3"><span className="text-amber-500">👉</span> <span>Take the job. Quit the job.</span></div>
  <div className="flex items-center gap-3"><span className="text-amber-500">👉</span> <span>Pick up a camera. Start writing. Build something.</span></div>
  <div className="flex items-center gap-3"><span className="text-amber-500">👉</span> <span>Move to another city.</span></div>
  <div className="flex items-center gap-3"><span className="text-amber-500">👉</span> <span>Have an embarrassing first attempt.</span></div>
  <div className="flex items-center gap-3"><span className="text-amber-500">👉</span> <span>Do something badly enough that you eventually become good at it.</span></div>

Because sometimes the thing you’re looking for isn’t hidden somewhere inside you waiting to be discovered.

<div className="p-6 my-8 border-l-4 border-orange-500 bg-orange-500/10 rounded-r-xl font-serif text-xl sm:text-2xl font-bold dark:text-orange-300 leading-relaxed">
  Sometimes you have to do things until you discover it.
</div>

And that’s why *Wake Up Sid* will probably remain one of my favourite movies.

Not because Sid figured everything out. **But because he finally decided to start figuring it out.**

And honestly, I think most of us are still somewhere between those two moments.
