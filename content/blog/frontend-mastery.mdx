  color="blue" 
/>

<div className="p-4 border-l-4 border-black dark:border-venom-slime bg-black/5 dark:bg-white/5 font-serif text-lg sm:text-xl font-medium leading-relaxed my-8">
  Excellence is the sum of small, deliberate engineering choices.
</div>

To prevent regressions while shipping continuous updates:

- **Self-Hosted Variable Typography**: Load fonts locally with `next/font` to eliminate render-blocking third-party network waterfalls.
- **Strict Image Dimensions**: Explicitly specify aspect ratios on all visual assets to mathematically prevent Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS).
- **Reduced Motion Fallbacks**: Wrap keyframe animations inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` to support users with vestibular sensitivity.
- **Dynamic Imports**: Lazy-load heavy visualization libraries (such as Three.js or Mermaid) so initial HTML bundles remain under 80 kilobytes.
